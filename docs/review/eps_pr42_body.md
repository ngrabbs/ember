Develops one connected EPS Rev B schematic for recovery power, source/output protection, STM32/CAN supervision, LTC4162 charging, BQ40Z50-R2 pack protection, isolated BMS communication power, buffered UART, supply-qualified SWD and four rail-measurement channels. The current development design has **14 sheets,345 physical parts,186 nets and933 physical endpoints**. Rev A remains the preserved bring-up baseline.

The new rail-telemetry block connects PA0/PA1/PA4/PA5 through a dedicated supply-qualified TMUX1511 and loaded resistor dividers. Filters sit before the isolation switch; MCU-side bleeds define the disconnected nodes. SYS/battery readings are diagnostic and do not replace BMS cell measurements or autonomous protection. Calculations include resistor/temperature corners, switch resistance, filter settling and an independent nodal check. Reference accuracy, ADC/switch dynamics and brownout/injection behavior still require acceptance.

Full-root development checks preserve the318 preceding physical components and verify the15 new net sets and173 functional-net classes. ERC retains **6 errors and2 existing warnings**: three unfinished MCU pins, three prior intentional pin-type joins and the two documented switched-VTref warnings. No types/severities are weakened or new exclusions added. Rendered-sheet inspection is recorded separately from electrical checks; remaining inherited presentation/tool limits are explicit.

Essential3V3 protection studies retain rejected and nonconvergent attempts. The downstream filter improves typical load-step behavior, but neither investigated input limiter is selected. Added source inductance reduces modeled pass-FET current while imposing about63 A on its magnetic and about20 mJ positive port energy on the FET. Physical magnetic/source bounds, controller voltage range, low-input drive, interruption/clamps and hot linear SOA remain open; favorable voltage traces do not establish survival.

The mechanical contract preserves EMBER's5.08 mm H1/H2 spacing and the confirmed16 mm clear facing-board gap. Actual COMMS CAN pins still require a coordinated peer revision to the canonical stack map. Courtyard inventory is an area budget, not placement or fit acceptance; the confirmed May board STEP omits holder/cells, which are visible in the separate Onshape assembly. Quantified holder clearance, mating tolerances and cable/harness clearance remain open.
