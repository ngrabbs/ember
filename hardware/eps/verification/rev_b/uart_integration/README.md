## Remaining electrical conditions

Use 3.3 V TTL UART at 115200 baud initially, **not RS-232 levels**. Adapter VOH ≥2.4 V / VOL ≤0.4 V under load, input leakage ≤10 µA and connector/cable load ≤50 pF are development conditions. The buffer's **≤10 ns/V input transition** requirement must be measured at the actual pins; low baud rate and Schmitt action do not prove it. Absent adapter holds MCU RX in BREAK: firmware must qualify idle-high and ignore absent-port errors without interrupt/wakeup storms. No firmware work is claimed.

The [parent-owned calculation](../../../verification/controller_rev_b/uart_screen/calculated.json) screens static margins and a 1.2 mA static budget; dynamic current is additional. Ioff ≤2 µA applies at **VCC=0 only**. The approximately 0.136 V output / 0.273 V rail bleed calculations are conditional leakage-budget screens, not proof through the undefined 0–1.65 V supply range, brownout, rail-floating transients, other interfaces or whole-EPS RBF. Connector ESD/abuse, startup waveform, harness clearance and all earlier BMS/RBF/power/SOA gates remain open.
