Local converter input capacitance is 98.1 µF nominal and the buck adds 4.5 µF, giving **102.6 µF nominal before upstream circuitry**, not a guaranteed effective or maximum source capacitance. Source-impedance, input inrush and simultaneous startup are not qualified by connecting the schematics. The prior source fixture/capacitance budget must be reconciled with this complete load and future charger/protection banks.

The corrected output bank remains eight CGA5L1X7R1H106K160AC 10 µF/50 V ceramics and five EEHZA1H680P 68 µF/50 V hybrids: **420 µF nominal, 496 µF initial high tolerance and 618.4 µF endurance-plus-tolerance sensitivity, plus C515** (100 nF/50 V). The 650 µF development allocation is conditional; it is not a guaranteed cold/bias/aging maximum. Physical effective capacitance, ESR, pulse endurance, area and height remain open. The 176 µF nominal 3V3 bank and controller bypass start from the same source but their combined input-current/time waveform has not been established.

The [current bank/loop record](../../protection/bank/README.md) and [latch follow-up](../../protection/latch/README.md) retain their stated model limits. The recovery converter starts autonomously through EN/UVLO with EXTVCC intentionally floating and MODE/DITH low. Actual COMMS/IHU startup currents, controlled admission, combined loop stability, PFM/transition behavior, thermal path, low-energy restart and fault containment remain unqualified. The 2 A case is a development sizing target. Neither independent converter study establishes simultaneous heavy startup, a protected external +5V supply or complete EPS essential-power acceptance.
