# Source-interlock gate screening

2026-10-23. **No RBF circuit acceptance or native implementation.** This follow-up narrows the earlier [source-isolation proposal](../../../design/rev_b_source_isolation_study.md). The hardware must block battery and solar in both directions without firmware, default off with either permission loop open, and preserve permitted depleted-pack recovery.

[Analytical script](screen_gate_bias.py) produces [results](analytical_results.json). For a common-source P-channel pair with 220 kΩ gate/source and 10 kΩ contact-loop pulldown, include both resistor tolerances and TCRs. The device's common-source voltage must exceed the calculated minimum to guarantee 4.5 V gate drive; a nominal 5 V cell pack does not establish that voltage after BMS/shunt/FET drops. A 4.5 V input sensitivity does not establish full-load resistance. The simple divider, resistor power, RC and capacitor-energy calculations are screening evidence only.

SUD50P08-25L-E3 is an 80 V P-channel candidate with 4.5 V drive data and a DPAK package. Its manufacturer top-view G/D/S and drain-tab drawing was actually inspected. Package/pad/disposable-render and procurement acceptance remain open. Si7455DP was also examined and is unsuitable as a drop-in low-pack candidate: its published resistance is guaranteed at 6 V/10 V drive, not 4.5 V. Both sources are linked below.

A gate/drain slew capacitor can hold the gate low during a source step and cause an unintended pulse; it cannot be accepted merely because normal turn-on is slow. A gate/source capacitor follows source movement, but increasing it also slows passive turn-off. Leakage, Miller coupling, contact bounce, repeated release/re-actuation and minimum-temperature gate behavior require explicit checks. Additional active gate-drive or separately interlocked precharge remains a candidate only; it would require an updated power-boundary and recovery analysis before selection.

Four LTspice runs using the unmodified manufacturer's SUD50P08 model failed to complete. The initial NPN-assisted concept, Gear variant, alternate-solver/ESR variant and simpler passive variant repeatedly relaxed tolerances at collapsing time steps. **No output peaks, inrush, cutoff times, MOSFET survival or pass/fail electrical limits were accepted.** [Failed-run record](failed_runs.json) preserves deck/model/archive/log hashes and the last observed solver positions. The small portable `.cir` files preserve those unsuccessful inputs, not validated designs. Generic NPN/diode models are explicitly unselected sensitivities.

For reproduction, download the [manufacturer ZIP](https://www.vishay.com/docs/77820/sud50p08.zip), verify its recorded hash and place its unmodified `sud50p08-25L_PS.lib` at `vendor/sud50p08.lib` beside the decks. Use LTspice 26.0.2 serially; an observation timeout is not a successful simulation. Vendor models and large incomplete raw/log files remain outside the repository. The first run was manually terminated; the others reached their process timeout.

Next acceptance depends on the actual charger/PowerPath input bank and BMS thresholds/delays, not only the downstream converter output capacitance. The integrated converters already total 102.6 µF nominal on their shared input; the complete upstream network is unfinished. Do not freeze the interlock capacitor/inrush or FET SOA budget until those paths are reconciled.

Sources: [SUD50P08-25L datasheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/73443/sud50p08.pdf), [Si7455DP datasheet](https://www.vishay.com/docs/73430/si7455dp.pdf), [manufacturer SPICE guide](https://www.vishay.com/docs/73498/73498.pdf). These component references do not establish flight qualification.
