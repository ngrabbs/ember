# Rev B UART debug interface

2026-10-24. Development circuit definition for PA2/PA3 USART2 AF1; no change to CAN or stack interfaces. Native build and physical-map acceptance are tracked in the subsequent UART integration checkpoint. This document alone is not proof of implementation.

Use Nexperia **74LVC2G34GW,125**, SOT363-2, with two independent noninverting buffers powered from permitted essential `+3V3`. Pin 1→6 carries MCU PA2 TX toward the connector; pin 3→4 carries connector RX toward MCU PA3. Supply pins are 5 and 2. Reuse the stock symbol and land only after exact manufacturer-view/pad mapping and disposable verification. Fit a local 100 nF bypass.

Use keyed JST **BM03B-GHS-TBT(LF)(SN)**, with the board header electrical numbering: 1 GND, 2 EPS TX output, 3 EPS RX input. Verify the actual component/mating drawing; this is not a universal UART cable pinout. The header provides **no supply pin**. The adapter must be independently powered and ground referenced, with compatible logic; do not connect RS-232 voltage levels or a supply lead.

Keep `EPS_UART_TX` and `EPS_UART_RX` as the MCU-to-debug-sheet global signals. Local names are `UART_TX_BUF`, `UART_TX_CONN`, `UART_RX_CONN` and `UART_RX_BUF`. These do not allocate stack pins. Put 330 Ω between TX buffer output and connector, and 47 Ω between connector RX and its buffer input. Each buffer output has a 68 kΩ pull-down. MCU-side TX input has a 100 kΩ pull-up to permitted `+3V3`; connector-side RX input has a **100 kΩ pull-down to GND**, avoiding a direct externally driven RX-to-supply resistor path. Add a separate 68 kΩ `+3V3`-to-GND bleed to drain residual UART-only off-state leakage. Draw all local paths with real wires and local supply/ground symbols.

The pull-down means an absent adapter reads as continuous BREAK on MCU RX. Firmware must qualify idle-high before enabling the console, ignore an absent-port BREAK, and avoid repeated error interrupts or unintended wakeups. TX stays idle-high after firmware initialization; neither console operation nor fault clearing may bypass RBF or autonomous protection. No firmware implementation is claimed here.

The [Nexperia manufacturer datasheet](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/74LVC2G34.pdf), Rev 13, Tables 6–8, provides static thresholds across 2.7–3.6 V, 2 µA maximum off-state leakage and output levels at a 100 µA load. [ST DS13560 Rev 6](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32g0b1ke.pdf), Tables 55/57, supplies MCU levels. The [calculation](../verification/controller_rev_b/uart_screen/calculated.json) conservatively screens resistor tolerance/TCR, inherited steady rail limits, output loading and logic margins. Under the stated current-budget assumption, the 68 kΩ bleeds give about 0.136 V per output and 0.273 V at the rail for two UART terminals. The datasheet specifies Ioff at VCC = 0 V; these calculated nonzero voltages are conditional screens, not guaranteed bounds across the undefined low-supply region. This is not zero leakage and does not include other external interfaces. It does not establish shutdown discharge time or transient behavior through the undefined-supply region.

Initial adapter requirements: 3.3 V UART, output high at least 2.4 V and low at most 0.4 V under the circuit load; input high/low thresholds compatible with 2.0/0.8 V; static input load no more than 10 µA; connector loading no more than 50 pF. Use 115200 baud as a development starting point. The buffer's ≤10 ns/V input transition requirement must be met at its actual pins; a low baud rate or Schmitt-action description does not waive it. Check the adapter, cable and PCB waveforms, overshoot, clock error and frame error rate before accepting the interface. Allocate 1.2 mA static supply current conservatively, including potential intermediate-input current; dynamic current is additional.

The interface is for controlled bench access, with connector ESD/abuse, brownout/back-power transients, harness clearance and assembly acceptance still open. SWD remains a separate unfinished interface and is not protected by these UART buffers. Full-EPS inhibit and flight qualification remain separate gates.
