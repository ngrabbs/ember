# BMS gate-stress containment candidate

2026-10-24. **Open blocking design gate; no additional gate-source clamp is installed by this record.** The 233-part BMS development checkpoint is not accepted as complete pack protection.

TI BQ40Z50-R2 [SLUSCS4C §7.18](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/bq40z50-r2.pdf) specifies on-drive relative to BAT, while DSG off-drive is relative to PACK. With the illustrative raw pack at 8.526 V and the published 12 V upper boost, the drive can approach 20.526 V above raw return. If an output short forces the main DSG source near zero before cutoff, Q201 VGS could exceed its CSD18543Q3A ±20 V absolute rating. The BAT blocking-diode drop reduces available drive by an unqualified amount; do not rely on it as a guaranteed clamp. This is a stress screen, not a coupled waveform result.

The grounded-gate reverse-connection transistor Q203 has source at PROTECTED_PACK and drain at the DSG gate. At a nonnegative shorted port its VGS is near zero, so it does not solve this positive-gate stress. Its reverse-port diagnostic is a separate test. Review rapid raw-battery collapse against the CHG source and held BAT reservoir as well; both main gates may warrant local clamps.

Evaluate **BZT52C15-7-F**, cathode to the affected gate and anode to that FET's actual source. This would clamp positive VGS and forward-clamp negative VGS. It is a stock-symbol/SOD123 candidate, not a newly approved or integrated component. The gate/source lead loop must be local to the physical FET, and the branch must not cross the RBF boundary.

The retrieved primary [Diodes DS18004 Rev 38, October 2017](https://www.diodes.com/datasheet/download/BZT52Cxx.pdf), p2, was actually rendered and viewed. BZT52C15 is 13.8–15.6 V at 5 mA, with 0.1 µA maximum reverse current specified at 10.5 V, and a listed 9.2–13.0 mV/°C coefficient at 5 mA. A first additive +60 K screen gives 16.38 V from the high test voltage. Those conditions do **not** establish dynamic clamping at another current or a guaranteed coefficient over the entire electronics range. Confirm the currently applicable commercial order-code specification before freezing it; the public primary PDF remains old and the live product-page fetch was unavailable.

A stiff 20.526 V driver through a minimum-temperature/tolerance-adjusted 5.1 kΩ resistor would feed roughly 1 mA into a 15–16 V clamp. Its 5 mA test voltage is therefore useful for screening, but the low-current knee, normal-on leakage, charge-pump loading, capacitance, turn-off delay and Miller/inductive pulse current need explicit checks. Do not substitute a generic SPICE zener and call that the manufacturer's response. Gate protection must preserve adequate enhancement at the lowest allowed pack voltage and autonomous fault recovery.

The unmodified TI CSD18543Q3A PSpice model is available from [SLPM286](https://www.ti.com/lit/zip/slpm286); the exact extracted input was `/tmp/ember-CSD18543Q3A.LIB` during this session. Verify its terminal declaration, compatibility and hash before a coupled run. No coupled BQ/main-FET or hot-SOA result is claimed here. A 35 V charger-to-pack fault and BMS PACK/BAT/VCC pin limits remain a separate containment requirement; a gate clamp cannot close them.

The subsequent [gate/channel sensitivity matrix](../verification/bms/gate_stress/README.md) establishes TI model compatibility and records 18 attempted cases: 12 complete and six rejected numerical failures. Its hypothetical clamp lowers gate voltage, while substantial channel dissipation remains. The lower-resistance short fixture reaches 20.36 V positive DSG VGS without the hypothetical clamp and produces negative gate excursions as well. This is an imposed-drive diagnostic with explicit omissions, not a coupled BQ model, a selected physical clamp, a survival result or authorization to close the gate. Continue local positive/negative protection checks for both main FETs and couple them with cutoff timing and SOA.

The later [physical-clamp evaluation](../verification/bms/physical_gate_clamp/README.md) resolves the primary BZT52C15 model download and documents a token-preserving syntax adapter. With Q203 and the 2 mΩ return shunt included, the nominal 25 °C imposed-drive comparison reduces DSG peak VGS from 20.21 V to 14.79 V. The previous large negative excursions disappear in this updated fixture and must not be presented as native-circuit predictions. Channel power remains roughly 724 W, so gate protection does not close survival. The model's 5 mA temperature slope is materially below the datasheet range; hot/cold clamping and normal-on pump loading remain unaccepted. Keep BZT52C15-7-F as the preferred development candidate, with both independent local gate/source clamps under evaluation; no production selection or native addition is made by this record.
