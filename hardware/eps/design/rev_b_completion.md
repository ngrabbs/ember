## Design sequence

The earlier CSD18543Q3A [PowerPath package checkpoint](../verification/charging/powerpath_package/README.md) now passes development physical mapping under the approved overlapping-rectangle construction: nine copper regions, five logical numbers, twelve paste apertures and nine separate mask openings. The stock source-pin stack and five drain aliases are preserved; library, live and saved-board geometry agree. Internal concave fillets, default reference placement and assembly/thermal qualification remain explicit review items. That package checkpoint added no real charger circuit; the subsequent charger integration now uses it. No real PCB placement was added.

The [Rev B root](../rev_b/eps_rev_b.kicad_pro) now includes unique Charger PowerPath and 2S pack-protection sheets with Controller, CAN, Essential 3V3, Recovery 5V converter, Solar source protection and Recovery rail protection. The BMS-stage graph contains 233 unique physical parts and 133 nets (115 functional), with 648 unique physical ref.pin endpoints. All 187 previous physical UUIDs and 359 earlier symbol objects remain, except five explicitly corrected resistor TCR fields documented in the [metadata correction](rev_b_passive_metadata_correction.md). ERC retains 11 unfinished MCU pin errors and three deliberate datasheet-derived output joins, with no warnings. Current electrical evidence is in the [BMS integration checkpoint](../verification/rev_b/bms_integration/README.md); rendered-sheet review is a separate gate. Resolve BMS gate stress and bus-power measurement, then hardware source isolation, stack interfaces and managed battery distribution. PROTECTED_PACK remains distinct from BATT_PERMITTED across the missing RBF stage. Shared startup, charger/filter behavior and pack-fault containment remain open. Retain historical studies; review the complete schematic before PCB synchronization. The 233-part area budget still omits unfinished blocks.

## Hardware acceptance inputs

Physical Rev A thermistor continuity and fitted values remain unknown. Final design ratings also need measured COMMS/IHU/payload idle, peak and inrush currents, solar source/harness limits, accepted cell specification and pack temperatures, and current holder/upper-board geometry. Development assumptions can support analysis and layout exploration; they do not establish these inputs.

The release remains open until source cutoff/back-power, charging and sensor faults, autonomous pack protection, A-to-B bus recovery, controlled startup, transient response, thermal behavior and mechanical clearance meet recorded limits. Flight acceptance additionally depends on the selected dispenser/launch requirements and environmental qualification.
