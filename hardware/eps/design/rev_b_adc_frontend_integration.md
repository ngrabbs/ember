## Electrical screen and operating conditions

Primary references: [ST DS13560 Rev6](https://www.st.com/resource/en/datasheet/stm32g0b1ke.pdf), ADC §5.3.18 and package pin tables; [TI TMUX1511 RevB](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tmux1511.pdf), §5.3/5.5/5.7 and application diagrams; [TPS3839](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps3839.pdf), as recorded in the existing SWD contract. Root inspected TMUX p21 functional diagram; inspect the relevant application figure and ST ADC connection drawing as well before final sheet acceptance. This circuit adapts the switch as four analog isolation paths, not the manufacturer's digital example or a complete ADC reference design.

`verification/controller_rev_b/adc_frontend/calculate.py` sweeps independent 0.1%+25 ppm×65K resistor corners and 0–4.5 Ω switch resistance. Intended input ceilings are60 V on SYS/BATT,6 V on protected+5V and3.6 V on protected+3V3. The high-rail dividers stay below3.6 V even with the switch open; loaded ADC maxima are2.347/1.727/1.601/2.347 V respectively, below the qualifier's2.857 V minimum falling threshold. This is conditional on valid regulator barriers/input ceilings, not survival of arbitrary or coincident faults. SYS/BATT upper resistors split voltage stress. Their coarse≈22.6 mV/LSB at3.6 V reference is acceptable for diagnostic path voltage; BMS telemetry remains authoritative for battery cell/pack protection and precision measurement. Slow scans must also implement ST's required ADC rearm behavior after long idle intervals; a10 Hz schedule alone does not establish correct ADC operation.

Worst calculated source impedance is8.625 kΩ. Use160.5-cycle sampling at≤35 MHz, configure PA0/PA1/PA4/PA5 analog/no pulls, disable the analog booster at VDDA≥2.4 V per ST, calibrate after power-up and honor ADC regulator/reference startup. Wait≥2 ms after switch qualification or a large rail transition before accepting a reading; the calculated filter settling sensitivities are<0.903 ms. Include a discarded conversion after channel changes and an initially slow10 Hz scan. Validate settling/crosstalk on hardware. The 5 pF internal sampling capacitance is typical, not a guaranteed external charge-kickback bound. ADC total error and reference/VREFINT errors remain separate; sampling or averaging does not remove them. Compute VDDA using a calibrated reference/VREFINT rather than claiming a divider of its own supply independently measures absolute3V3.

TMUX's2 µA maximum powered-off leakage atVDD=0 would produce≤94.25 mV into the47 kΩ bleed **in that test condition**. It does not establish leakage throughout a nonzero brownout, nor all MCU/EPS power-off feeds. ON leakage and MCU TT_a leakage figures are conditional; the0.2 µA combined sensitivity is an accuracy term, not a guaranteed all-state bound. The dedicated supervisor has the same typical-only assertion delay/hysteresis limitations recorded for SWD. No firmware permission enables the analog switch. Rapid/slow collapse, switch timing, capacitor charge, ADC injection, sensor faults, exact reference accuracy and full off-state equilibrium remain hardware/electrical acceptance gates.
