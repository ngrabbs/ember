# Stage 2 — three autonomous AON bootstrap paths

2026-10-05. Actual wired schematic capture, not a fabrication or flight release. This stage extends the stage1 AON buck and reset/run core with `02_bootstrap_inputs.kicad_sch`. The converted carrier and older fixtures remain preserved. Main admissions, MCU/watchdog, source lock, module and camera circuitry are still future work.

Each source now has a TPS26600PWPR with autonomous initial startup, adjustable overvoltage cutoff, active current limiting with auto-retry, reverse-polarity protection and native reverse-blocking OR. All three OUT15/16 pairs join the actual AON_BOOTSTRAP_IN hierarchy connection to LMR36506 VIN/EN and its switched10uF+100nF input capacitance. No additional Schottky drop is used. UVLO3 joins each branch's own IN1/2, allowing initial USB5V; RTN would select a roughly15V factory threshold and is deliberately not used there. SHDN7 is open/self-biased; FLT14 and IMON10 are intentionally unused. NC4/13 keep their library no_connect electrical type without visually confusing additional X marks.

Every branch's RILIM, CdVdt, OV divider bottom, MODE6, RTN8 and EP17 connect to its own visible RTN bus. Those three buses are separate from systemGND and one another. GND9 and raw input capacitor returns use systemGND. No power-driver flags hide missing external sources or returns. The existing AON flag declares only the real buck output.

The provisional prototype entry fault envelope is at most24V magnitude. It is an explicit qualification constraint, not a built entry protector or a known EPS specification. At +24V the OV sense dividers remain below the4V recommended sense limit;50V raw/supply capacitors are not a claim that the board survives60V faults. Coordinated fuse clearing energy, TVS dynamic clamp, cable/source impedance and connector current ratings must be closed before building or accepting unrestricted source operation. Protection failure and physical flight isolation remain separate requirements.

USB OV resistors130k/10k, bench/stack66.5k/10k, each with a provisional ±1% total resistance allocation, yield conservative rising ranges16.066–17.485V and8.790–9.543V including sense leakage and full-temperature threshold endpoints. Generic passive values/footprints are capture allocations; exact resistor tolerance/TCR, capacitor effective capacitance and assembly sourcing remain required. The bootstrap OV thresholds do not replace the tighter future main-source valid windows.

RILIM118k±1% stays within TI's120k recommended maximum. Inverse scaling of TI's120k row gives an **inferred**, not separately guaranteed,85.6–118.1mA overload and80.6–123.3mA short range. This is below the selected >=500mA initial USB source contract but does not support arbitrary100mA legacy USB hosts. Transient peaks/fast-trip delay are not bounded by the steady short range.

CdVdt220nF with ±20% effective allocation gives about360–797V/s; charging the12.12uF maximum switched input capacitance requires at most9.66mA at the ramp end. `check_stage2.py` reproduces a conditional4.35V buck-input/80% efficiency budget, a provisional4mA PD reserve and1.17mA three-eFuse quiescent reserve. The latter is a budget reserve: TI's enabled Iq row does not guarantee the inactive OUT-biased branches' consumption in this exact OR configuration. At0.200W regulated load the conditional ramp-end sum is about72.3mA;0.250W reaches86.7mA, exceeding the inferred minimum overload.0.350W cannot be assumed available at initial5V.

**These sums do not prove cold startup.** LMR36506 is VIN-enabled during the eFuse ramp, so its initial low-voltage input current and output-cap charging require captures at worst source impedance/minimum limit, initial5V,5→15V PD change, retry and brownout. The250mΩ maximum switch resistance is specified at >=100mA and is not claimed at the nominal bootstrap load; actual low-load drop and minimum buck-input voltage require measurement. Additions to downstream AON capacitance invalidate the ramp estimate. The direct raw USB capacitance captured here is100nF; future PD/main-path capacitors must fit the **whole receptacle** attach budget.

Native OR follows TI TPS2660 §10.3 and avoids direct source tying. It still has nonzero reverse leakage and finite response. Existing VIN0/SHDN0 leakage rows do not qualify the self-biased absent-source case. Test absent/negative/falling feeds, retained output and simultaneous-source transitions, possible sense-rail charging, bleed needs and thermal behavior. Native OR is not a physical launch inhibit, and bootstrap OR does not authorize main automatic source failover.
